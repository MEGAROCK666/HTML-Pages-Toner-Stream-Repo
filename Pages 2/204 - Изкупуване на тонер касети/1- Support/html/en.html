Toner Cartridge Buyback

Toner Cartridge Buyback Conditions

We only buy „virgin“ cartridges – cartridges that have been used only once, without being refilled or otherwise modified.

Important: Toner Support Ltd. reserves the right to determine whether the empty toner cartridges you offer will be accepted for buyback.
2

What Does OEM Mean?

* OEM – Original Equipment Manufacturer. These are products with verified manufacturer origin that are not counterfeit.

3

What Is an MFP?

** MFP – multifunction printer.

4

Types of Toner Cartridges We DO NOT Buy

  1. Compatible toner cartridges („clones“) – low-quality, single-use products that often infringe patents and are harmful to the environment.
  2. Starter toner cartridges – cartridges supplied with a brand-new printer or MFP, usually with a lower capacity and different construction.
5

Buyback Prices

The prices listed on the website apply to quantities of 1 to 20 units. For larger quantities or rare models, prices are subject to negotiation. All prices exclude VAT and transportation costs.

6

Payment Methods

  • Individuals: payment in cash at our office or by money transfer.
  • Legal entities: payment in cash, by money transfer, or by bank transfer.
7

Buyback Process

Our specialists inspect each cartridge to ensure that it is „virgin“ and suitable for buyback. For large quantities, we recommend contacting us in advance.

8

On-Site Buyback in Sofia

Visit us at: 14A Prof. Ivan Georgov St., Sofia, every business day from 09:00 to 18:00.

9

Collection with Our Transport (Sofia)

For large quantities, we can provide transportation. If the cartridges do not meet the requirements, a transportation fee of 5.00 € will be deducted from the amount payable.

10

Shipping from Other Parts of Bulgaria via Econt

Send the cartridges to the Econt „Sofia Voenna Rampa“ office, marked „with inspection upon delivery“. If the value of the cartridges exceeds 100.00 € excluding VAT, we cover the transportation costs.
