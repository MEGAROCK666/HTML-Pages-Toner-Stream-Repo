Careers at TonerStream

Professionalism, Stability, and Long-Term Development

TonerStream is an established Bulgarian company specializing in the supply of printing consumables, servicing of printing and laser equipment, and providing comprehensive solutions for small and large businesses. We work with end customers and corporate partners, focusing on high quality, expertise, and integrity in every aspect of our work.

Who We Are

TonerStream is a reliable B2C partner with a long-standing presence on the Bulgarian market. The company offers:

  • Original and compatible toner cartridges;
  • Toner cartridge manufacturing;
  • Service and technical support for printing equipment;
  • Printer and copier rental and refurbished equipment;
  • Consulting and print cost optimization;
  • Professional service through an online platform and sales team.

Why Work at TonerStream

  • A stable and professional working environment;
  • Clear duties and responsibilities;
  • Opportunities for professional development;
  • Work with real business clients and projects;
  • A team of experienced professionals;
  • Compensation based on contribution and experience.
