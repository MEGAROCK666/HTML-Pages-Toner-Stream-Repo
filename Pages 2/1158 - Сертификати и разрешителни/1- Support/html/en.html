At TONER SUPPORT we believe that high quality goes hand in hand with responsibility towards both nature and the customers.
All our products are developed and offered with care, precision, and a strong commitment to sustainable growth.

We strictly comply with European regulations and standards related to product safety, intellectual property protection, human health, and environmental preservation.

As early as 2013, we implemented a Quality Management System ISO 9001 and an Environmental Management System ISO 14001, ensuring that every product we offer meets the highest requirements.

In addition, all our Safety Data Sheets (SDS) are prepared in accordance with the European REACH regulation and are available for every product.

Our environmental commitment and dedication to sustainable development led us to become the first Bulgarian company to join ETIRA (The European Toner and Inkjet Remanufacturers Association). This is a testament to our mission – to provide reliable, high-quality, and environmentally responsible printing solutions.
