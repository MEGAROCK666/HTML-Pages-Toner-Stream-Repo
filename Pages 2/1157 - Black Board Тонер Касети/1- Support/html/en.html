BLACKBOARD is a trading brand, made by TONER SUPPORT, for toner cartridges.
Registered in 2010, BLACKBOARD cartridges are produced from original empties and with
completely new mechanics from selected leading manufacturers brands.
Before being released on the market, each toner cartridge model goes through a
long test period until impeccable quality is achieved.
The packaging is excellently branded and checks every box so that your
packages get to your home safely.
Thanks to the strict manufacturing concept and adherence to high
quality standards, BLACKBOARD are so durable that they can be rebuilt
over and over again.
The BLACKBOARD toner cartridges have all the qualities of their
original counterparts, and most importantly- made in Bulgaria!
