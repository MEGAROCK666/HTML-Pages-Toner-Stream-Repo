Water Foam Roller – CET Features

  • Product Highlights:

    Stable and Consistent Shape: CET's product is cured to achieve a 100-nanometer foam structure, ensuring that the roller maintains a consistent and stable shape during use.

    Excellent Fusing Performance: The flexibility of the water-based foam material is twice that of conventional foam roller products, providing greater elasticity to restore its original shape and ensure excellent fusing performance.

    Extends the Life of the Fuser Assembly: CET rollers use a unique water-based foam structure that stores and releases heat more effectively, providing faster and more consistent performance while significantly extending the product's service life.

Kuroki Blades with the “2J” Designation

Kuroki blades with the “2J” designation are manufactured with a lubricant that:

  • Lasts for more than 50,000 pages.
  • Has been tested by OEM manufacturers and leading OPC drum manufacturers.
  • Provides additional protection for the blade and OPC drum, increasing their durability and performance.
  • Provides easy maintenance and installation and does not wear easily – even when touched.

What Does C-BOX Mean?

  • We offer products for both older machines and newer generations, ranging from small printers or fax machines for home use to large-format printing equipment. All Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) products we offer are also available in a budget category based on the condition of their packaging. This category is called C-BOX. These are boxes with cosmetic damage to their exterior that contain unopened, unused products, allowing you to save even more money. The C-BOX category includes boxes that may have 1 or 2 cosmetic imperfections, such as an attached label, a crushed corner, or a small marking on the box, as well as boxes with 3 or more cosmetic imperfections, which may include a hole, multiple shipping labels, multiple markings, or torn outer packaging, while the product inside remains intact and sealed. It is important to note that the box may still be unopened (boxes where the opening seal has not been removed). It may also be an opened box that is either in perfect condition or has visible defects, while the product inside remains sealed and in new (unused) condition.
