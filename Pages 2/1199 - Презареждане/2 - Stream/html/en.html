What Does Toner Cartridge Refilling Involve?

This is a procedure that includes several steps.

  1. The cartridge is disassembled and cleaned of any remaining old toner.
  2. The cartridge is then refilled with new toner.
  3. Once the cartridge has been filled with the required amount of toner, we reassemble it.
  4. The refilled toner cartridge is then tested to ensure that it is ready for use. The test includes checking print density and consistency, as well as overall functionality.
  5. After successful testing, the refilled toner cartridge is packaged and handed over to the customer fully ready for use. 

Is Toner Refilling a Good Solution?

Toner cartridge refilling is both an environmentally friendly and cost-effective solution. It allows a toner cartridge to be used for longer instead of constantly purchasing and replacing new consumables. It is also significantly more affordable than replacing the cartridge with an original toner cartridge.

However, toner refilling also has a disadvantage. During this process, worn components inside the toner cartridge are not replaced. To ensure high-quality and trouble-free printing, it is important to replace worn parts in a timely manner.
