Great news: every consumable you order is delivered FREE OF CHARGE to your address for orders within Sofia. Delivery is completed within 24 hours of receiving your order.

Delivery Throughout Bulgaria

For all other locations in Bulgaria, we offer Cash on Delivery. Once you send your consumable to us, we process it and return it to you by courier. You pay the transportation and service costs after you have seen the completed order. What could be better than that!

2

Order Online at Any Time

You can place your order online through our website whenever it is convenient for you. Our website also has a practical mobile version. This allows you to stay securely connected with us even outside our business hours, from anywhere in the world.

3

Order In Person

If you prefer to meet us in person and place your order directly, you are welcome to visit our office! We will do our best to process your consumable within 2 hours.
