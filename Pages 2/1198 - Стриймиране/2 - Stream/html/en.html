What Makes This Service Different

Restoring the Original Cartridge, Not Just Refilling It

Toner Stream's streaming service provides comprehensive restoration of original toner cartridges through diagnostics, replacement of worn components, toner filling and installation of a new chip.

The difference lies in the extent of restoration.

With conventional refilling the cartridge is cleaned and filled with toner. With streaming, its technical condition is checked first. Worn components are then replaced as needed, after which the cartridge is filled with toner and fitted with a new chip.

When diagnostics show that the components have reached the end of their service life, a more comprehensive restoration is carried out: recycling with replacement of all parts.

1
Diagnostics The cartridge's actual condition is assessed.
2
Condition-based replacement Not all components are replaced automatically.
3
Final quality check The restored cartridge is checked before delivery.

Extending the Life of Original Toner Cartridges

With streaming, the original cartridge is not discarded when the toner runs out. It is diagnosed and restored for reuse. Worn components are replaced, toner is added and a new chip is installed. This extends the life of the existing cartridge and reduces the need to manufacture and dispose of an entirely new consumable.

Reuse The original housing gets a new lease of life.
Replace Only What Is Needed Streaming does not automatically replace every component.
Less Waste The cartridge is restored and used again.

The Difference Between Streaming and Recycling

Streaming and recycling are two levels of the same original toner cartridge restoration process, differing in how many parts are replaced. Diagnostics determine which level is appropriate.

One Restoration Process Different Levels of Replacement
Streaming Only the necessary parts
Replacement of worn parts
Recycling All parts
Replacement of all parts
Both include: toner filling + new chip + final test
Streaming

Replacement of worn parts only

Scope

Only parts identified as worn are replaced. The cartridge is filled with toner and fitted with a new chip.

When is it suitable?

When the cartridge housing and main components are in good condition.

Result

A restored cartridge with the necessary components replaced and tested before delivery.

Recycling

Replacement of all parts

Scope

All cartridge parts are replaced, the cartridge is filled with toner and a new chip is installed.

When is it suitable?

When the components have reached the end of their service life and complete replacement is required.

Result

A fully restored cartridge that has passed final testing.

You do not need to decide which level of restoration is right for you.

Once we receive your toner cartridge, we diagnose it and determine the appropriate scope of restoration based on the condition of its housing and components.

By comparison, toner cartridge refilling is a more limited operation involving only filling with toner.

How We Check the Cartridge Before Delivery

You receive more than an old cartridge that has simply been refilled. The empty original cartridge undergoes diagnostics, cleaning, replacement of worn components and a final test before it is ready for use.

1

We Replace Worn Components

During streaming, components identified as worn during diagnostics are replaced.

  • Drums
  • Charge rollers
  • Cleaning blades
  • Chips
2

We Check the Cartridge's Condition

Before processing, the cartridge is diagnosed to assess its current technical condition and identify components requiring replacement.

After cleaning and restoration, a final test is performed before the cartridge is sealed.

3

We Check Print Quality

The final test is carried out before delivery to check the cartridge's technical condition.

The aim is to avoid problems such as white lines, faint printing, black dots and background shading.

4

Predictable Page Yield

Page yield depends on the specific model and its capacity. For streamed cartridges, Toner Stream specifies a yield comparable to that of a cartridge with the same capacity.

With streaming, we guarantee at least the same number of printed pages as you get with a manufacturer-branded consumable.

Warranty

12-Month Warranty on Every Purchased Consumable

Every purchased consumable comes with a 12-month warranty from the purchase date or until its rated capacity is exhausted.

Toner cartridge yield is calculated according to the ISO standard — 5% coverage on an A4 page, and is based on the figures quoted by the manufacturer, which we include in each product description.
