What Is Streaming?

Streaming is a process for maintaining and restoring toner cartridges that includes several steps:

  1. After you send us your empty original toner cartridge, we test it to identify the problem.
  2. We clean it and replace any worn parts.
  3. We refill it with toner.
  4. We package the cartridge after performing a final test to ensure that we provide a fully functional and reliable product.

By following this step-by-step process, we are able to provide our customers with high print quality at a competitive price.
